Life Timer shows the time you have left until age 100 in years, days, hours, minutes and seconds, ticking live on your screen.

Enter your birthday once and you're done. No account, no sign-up, no questionnaire. Your birthday stays on your device.

What you get
• A live countdown to age 100, down to the second
• A life-progress bar that shows how far along you are
• A short line of motivation, with another one a tap away
• Optional reminders three times a day (8 AM, 1 PM, 6 PM)
• Share your countdown with friends

Why people use it
Watching the seconds move is a quiet nudge to spend today on what matters. A calm memento mori and life clock, without the doom.

Please note
Life Timer uses a fixed 100-year reference. It does not use life expectancy statistics, does not predict your actual lifespan or health, and does not provide medical information.

The app is free and supported by ads.
